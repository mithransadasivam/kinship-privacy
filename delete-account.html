Delete Your Kinship Account

Developer: Mithran Sadasivam (SK Apps)
Contact: mithran.sadasivam@gmail.com

You can request deletion of your Kinship account and associated personal data at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to mithran.sadasivam@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Kinship account (the one you used to sign in with Google).
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my Kinship account."
  3. We will verify your request and process it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account information (name, email, Google account identifier).
  • Your profile information (phone number, address, if provided).
  • Family tree content you personally own (trees you created).
  • Your membership in any family trees you joined.

What may be retained

  • Family tree content you contributed to a tree owned by another user (e.g., relatives you added) may remain part of that tree, since it is shared content owned by the tree's creator, unless the tree owner also removes it.
  • Records we are required to keep for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes, retained only as long as necessary for that purpose.

If you have questions about this process, contact mithran.sadasivam@gmail.com.
