Kinship Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026
Developer: Mithran Sadasivam (SK Apps)
Contact: mithran.sadasivam@gmail.com

Kinship ("the app") lets you build and share a family tree with relatives. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and who it is shared with.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and a Google account identifier. We do not receive your Google password.

Profile information you provide. You may add a phone number and address to your profile.

Family tree content you enter. This includes names, phone numbers, email addresses, birth and death dates, birth and death places, and the relationships between family members. You may enter information about other people, such as relatives. Please only enter information you have a right to share.

Activity within the app. This includes family codes you generate, join requests and any message you include with them, tree membership, and an edit history of changes made to a tree.

Advertising data. The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, and device and app information to serve and measure ads.

2. How we use information

  • To create your account and keep you signed in.
  • To store, display, and sync your family trees across your devices.
  • To let tree owners approve or reject requests to join their tree.
  • To show an edit history so tree members can see who changed what.
  • To show ads, which help keep the app free.
  • To maintain security and fix problems.

3. Who can see your information

Information in a family tree is visible to the people who are members of that tree. When you join a tree, its owner and other members can see the tree contents and your name and email. Your information is not visible to other users outside the trees you belong to.

4. Sharing with third parties

  • Google: for sign-in (Google Sign-In) and advertising (AdMob). Google's privacy policy is at https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Railway: our hosting provider, which stores the app's server and database.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Data security

Data is sent between the app and our servers over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account exists. You can delete family members, relationships, and trees you own within the app. To delete your account and the personal data associated with it, email mithran.sadasivam@gmail.com from the address used to sign in, and we will process the request within a reasonable time.

7. Children

Kinship is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will remove it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows the latest version.

9. Contact

Questions or requests: mithran.sadasivam@gmail.com
